# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

*Nota: use el nombre exacto que aparece en el Certificate of Formation (NJ) o en el certificado de existencia y representación legal (Colombia). El UBI del cliente lo encuentras en la búsqueda de negocios del Secretary of State de Washington.*

## 1. Services
1.1 Provider will perform the digital marketing, advertising, web design, graphic design, and social media services described in each Statement of Work ("**SOW**") signed by both parties. Each SOW is governed by this Agreement.
1.2 If a SOW conflicts with this Agreement, the SOW controls only for that SOW and only if it expressly says so.
1.3 Work outside a SOW requires a written change order signed by both parties, with the added fees and timeline.

## 2. Client Responsibilities
2.1 Client will provide, on time, the content, logos, photos, service area information, pricing, licenses, and approvals that Provider reasonably needs.
2.2 Client designates [NAME, EMAIL] as its approver. Approvals by email are binding. If Client does not respond within [5] business days, deadlines move day for day.
2.3 Client is responsible for the truth of its business claims, including licensing, bonding, insurance, pricing, guarantees, and reviews, and for its compliance with Washington and federal consumer protection law.

## 3. Fees and Payment
3.1 Client will pay the fees in the SOW in U.S. dollars.
3.2 Unless a SOW says otherwise: fixed-fee projects are invoiced [50%] at signing and [50%] at delivery; monthly retainers are invoiced in advance on the [1st] of each month.
3.3 Invoices are due within [15] days. Late amounts bear interest at [1.5]% per month or the maximum allowed by law, if lower. Provider may suspend work if an invoice is more than [10] days overdue.
3.4 **Advertising spend.** Media spend on Meta, Google, and other platforms is not included in Provider's fees. Client will pay platforms directly through its own ad accounts and payment methods, and Client owns those accounts. Provider's access is as an authorized manager. If the parties agree that Provider advances ad spend, it will be re-billed at cost, due in advance.
3.5 **Third-party costs** (domains, hosting, plugins, stock media, software licenses, printing) are paid by Client directly or re-billed at cost with [10]% handling, only if approved in advance.
3.6 **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of sales, use, B&O pass-through, VAT, and similar transaction taxes. If any transaction tax applies to the Services, Provider will show it on the invoice when required by law and Client will pay it; if Client claims an exemption or is responsible for self-assessing use tax, Client will say so in writing. Each party is responsible for its own income taxes. Provider will deliver a [Form W-9 / Form W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E] to Client on request. If Client believes withholding is required by law, Client will notify Provider before payment and give a certificate of any amount withheld.
*Nota: en Washington, desde el 1 de octubre de 2025 los servicios de publicidad, de tecnología y de desarrollo de sitios web a medida pueden estar sujetos a retail sales tax. Ver la guía operativa, sección 4. Esta cláusula reparte la responsabilidad, pero no sustituye la revisión de un contador.*
3.7 Payments are non-refundable for work already performed, except as stated in Section 9.

## 4. Intellectual Property
4.1 **Client Materials.** Client keeps ownership of its names, logos, photos, content, and data ("**Client Materials**") and grants Provider a limited license to use them to perform the Services and to show the work in Provider's portfolio, unless Client opts out in writing.
4.2 **Deliverables.** Upon payment in full, Provider assigns to Client its rights in the final custom deliverables expressly listed in the SOW (logo files, graphics, custom website design, written copy), excluding Provider Tools.
4.3 **Provider Tools.** Provider keeps ownership of its pre-existing and general-purpose materials, templates, code libraries, processes, and know-how ("**Provider Tools**") and grants Client a non-exclusive, perpetual license to use them as embedded in the deliverables.
4.4 **Third-party and open-source materials** (WordPress, themes, plugins, fonts, stock images) stay under their own licenses. Client will keep any required paid licenses active.
4.5 Provider will not knowingly deliver material that infringes third-party rights. Client is responsible for Client Materials it provides.

## 5. Platform Rules and Compliance
5.1 Services depend on third-party platforms (Meta, Google, WordPress hosts, and others). Platform policies, algorithms, and account decisions can change or suspend ads or accounts. Provider is not responsible for platform decisions it does not control, but will help Client appeal them in good faith.
5.2 Both parties will follow applicable law for advertising and marketing, including FTC rules on endorsements and consumer reviews, the Washington Consumer Protection Act, CAN-SPAM, and the TCPA for calls and texts. Client will provide written proof of consent before Provider sends marketing messages to any person. Provider will not create fake reviews or testimonials.
5.3 Client is responsible for the accuracy of offers, "licensed, bonded, insured" statements, and "satisfaction guarantee" claims it asks Provider to publish.

## 6. No Guarantee of Results
Marketing results depend on factors outside Provider's control, including budget, market, seasonality, competition, and Client's service and pricing. Provider does not guarantee any number of leads, calls, rankings, clicks, followers, or revenue. Any targets in a SOW are goals, not promises.

## 7. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's non-public business, financial, and technical information confidential, use it only for this Agreement, and protect it with reasonable care. This does not apply to information that is public, already known, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice where permitted). This section survives for [3] years after termination.

## 8. Data and Privacy
8.1 Client is the owner and controller of its customer and lead data. Provider will use it only to provide the Services, keep it secure with reasonable safeguards, and notify Client without undue delay after learning of a breach affecting it.
8.2 Provider may process data in the United States and in Colombia, and will use service providers that protect data at a comparable level. Client will give its website visitors the privacy notices and cookie choices required by law.

## 9. Term and Termination
9.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until terminated.
9.2 Either party may terminate a monthly retainer SOW for convenience with [30] days' written notice. Fixed-fee SOWs may be ended only for cause, or by Client with payment for work performed plus [25]% of the remaining unpaid fees.
9.3 Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within [15] days after written notice.
9.4 On termination, Client pays all fees earned and costs incurred through the termination date. Provider will, within [10] days after payment, hand over website files, design source files for paid deliverables, and admin access to Client-owned accounts.

## 10. Warranties and Disclaimer
10.1 Each party has the authority to enter into this Agreement.
10.2 Provider will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner and will fix, at no charge, deliverables that do not match the SOW if Client reports them within [30] days of delivery.
10.3 EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 11. Limitation of Liability and Indemnity
11.1 NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS OR REVENUE, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THEIR POSSIBILITY.
11.2 PROVIDER'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IS LIMITED TO THE FEES CLIENT PAID TO PROVIDER IN THE [6] MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.
11.3 The limits in this Section do not apply to Client's payment obligations, a party's indemnity obligations, breach of confidentiality, or liability that cannot be limited by law.
11.4 Client will defend and indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from Client Materials, Client's business practices, or Client's services to its customers. Provider will defend and indemnify Client against third-party claims that Provider's original deliverables, as delivered, infringe a U.S. copyright or trademark.

## 12. Independent Contractor
Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing here creates an employment, partnership, agency, or joint venture relationship. Provider controls how, when, and where the Services are performed, using its own tools, and is responsible for its own personnel, taxes, and insurance. Provider performs the Services [from outside the United States / from New Jersey and remotely] and is not authorized to bind Client.

## 13. Insurance
Provider will maintain [professional liability (errors and omissions) insurance of at least USD [1,000,000] per claim] and will provide a certificate on request. Client will maintain commercial general liability insurance for its business.

## 14. Non-Solicitation (optional)
During the term and for [12] months after, neither party will directly solicit for hire the other party's employees or contractors who worked on the Services, except through general job postings.

## 15. Dispute Resolution, Governing Law
15.1 The parties will first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation for [30] days, then non-binding mediation by video conference.
15.2 If unresolved, the dispute will be decided by [binding arbitration by a single arbitrator under the AAA Commercial Rules, held by video conference / the state or federal courts located in King County, Washington]. The prevailing party recovers reasonable attorneys' fees.
15.3 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [Washington / New Jersey], without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. Either party may seek an injunction to protect confidential information or intellectual property.
*Nota: el cliente probablemente pida Washington; New Jersey te favorece si tu LLC está allá. Una cláusula intermedia común es arbitraje remoto con ley de Washington.*

## 16. General
16.1 **Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control, except payment obligations.
16.2 **Notices** are in writing and effective by email to the addresses on the signature page, with confirmation of receipt.
16.3 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor in a merger or sale of substantially all its business.
16.4 **Entire agreement; amendments.** This Agreement, with each SOW, is the entire agreement. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.
16.5 **Severability; waiver.** If a provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. A failure to enforce a right is not a waiver.
16.6 **Language.** This Agreement is in English. Any translation is for convenience only and the English text controls.
16.7 **Electronic signatures and counterparts.** The parties may sign electronically and in counterparts, and electronic signatures are as valid as ink under the ESIGN Act and the Washington Uniform Electronic Transactions Act.
*Nota: no necesitas notario para un contrato comercial entre empresas en EE. UU.; la firma electrónica (DocuSign, Dropbox Sign) basta. Ver la guía operativa, sección 6.*
